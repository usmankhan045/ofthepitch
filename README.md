# Of The Pitch

### Practical Guides to Going to Sport in Person

Of The Pitch is an independent sports and event guide built for people
who are actually attending sporting events.

The platform focuses on the details that matter once you decide to go:
tickets, seats, venue rules, dress codes, transport, accommodation and
the real cost of attending.

> **Of The Pitch is built and operated as an independent digital
> publication.**

---

## 🏟️ About Of The Pitch

Watching sport on television is simple.

Going to a major sporting event is not.

Of The Pitch focuses on the practical information people need before
they travel to a stadium, circuit, racecourse, resort or tournament.

The platform covers:

- Tickets
- Seating
- Enclosures
- Venue rules
- Dress codes
- Travel
- Accommodation
- Event logistics
- Costs
- Matchday planning
- Sporting event experiences

## 🔎 Research Approach

Of The Pitch focuses on information that affects the actual experience
of attending an event.

Guides investigate questions such as:

- What does the ticket actually include?
- Where can you sit?
- What rules are enforced at the venue?
- Is there actually a dress code?
- How do you get to the venue?
- Where should you stay?
- What does the full day actually cost?
- What should a first-time visitor know?

Venue rules and event information are checked against published
guidance where possible, with dates included because event policies,
ticket terms and requirements can change. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
